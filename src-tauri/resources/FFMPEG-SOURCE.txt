FFmpeg in Resize Video: licence and source
==========================================

Resize Video renders with a separate program, ffmpeg.exe, installed next to
resize-video.exe. That program is not our work. It is FFmpeg, and this copy was
built with GPL components enabled, so it is distributed under the GNU General
Public License, version 3. The full licence text is in COPYING.GPLv3, in this
folder. You may copy and redistribute ffmpeg.exe under those terms.

Where the corresponding source is
---------------------------------

FFmpeg itself: the source of exactly this build is FFmpeg commit f22fcd4483
(17 December 2018), available from the official repository:

  https://git.ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.git
  https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/commit/f22fcd4483fca8dda10e035906b8f8bc60f8b69c

Build with the options in the "Configuration" line above.

The libraries linked into the build (x264, x265, libvpx, LAME, Opus and the
others named in the configuration line) are separate projects, each distributed
by its own authors. The exact versions that went into the 20181217 build are
not recorded in this repository. If you need the sources of those components
as they were in this binary, ask for them (below); do not assume the current
releases are the same.

Asking for the source
---------------------

If you received Resize Video from your team, ask the people who gave you the
installer. They are obliged to give you the corresponding source of the
ffmpeg.exe in it, or to tell you where to get it, on request.
